FLOORING
Types: Laminate (ground floor), Carpet (first floor)

ELECTRICAL
Condition: Poor
Notes: Consumer unit is original to the property (circa 1975) and does not

PLUMBING
Condition: Satisfactory
Notes: Boiler is 12 years old and approaching end of expected service life.
Hot water pressure is adequate. Minor drip from the kitchen tap washer.

SUMMARY
This property requires significant investment before it can be considered
move-in ready. The roof and electrical installation are the most urgent
concerns. Buyers should factor remedial costs of £10,000-£15,000 into
their offer price. A structural engineer's report is also recommended
given the extent of the damp ingress.
